Ereteti Dairy Sacco

Who we are

Ereteti Dairy Sacco is a farmers' co-operative. Around eight hundred farmers deliver milk twice a day to our collection centres.

The sacco weighs the milk, records it in a paper ledger, and pays once a month.

Notices

  • Milk is paid at KSh 52.00 a litre from 1 October 2026.
  • Annual General Meeting: Saturday 17 October, 10 a.m., at the Kiserian collection centre.
  • New members: apply to join. You need a national ID and a bank account.
